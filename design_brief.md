# DVIT Consulting Design Brief

## Brand Goal
DVIT Consulting helps small businesses feel confident that their technology is handled professionally.

The website should communicate:
- trust
- professionalism
- clarity
- responsiveness
- modern expertise
- business-friendly support

The website should feel like a reliable, modern IT consulting partner for small businesses.

## Target Audience
Small business owners, office managers, and decision-makers at businesses with roughly 10 to 100 employees.

They are not looking for flashy design.
They want:
- confidence
- competence
- easy communication
- clear services
- low friction contact

## Overall Visual Direction
- Modern MSP / SaaS-inspired layout
- Premium but approachable
- Calm and polished
- Clean whitespace
- Subtle motion
- Strong readability
- Designed to look trustworthy, not trendy for the sake of trendiness

## Aesthetic Keywords
- business-friendly
- modern
- premium
- clean
- calm
- trustworthy
- intentional
- clear
- polished

## Avoid
- hacker / cyberpunk aesthetics
- neon green or red “cybersecurity” styling
- overly dark interfaces
- excessive gradients everywhere
- too many glowing effects
- overly rounded childish UI
- cluttered layouts
- generic Tailwind-demo style sections
- visual gimmicks that distract from trust

## Color Direction
Use a blue / slate / white palette.

Preferred feel:
- strong primary blue
- slate neutrals
- soft off-white backgrounds
- subtle gradient accents only where useful
- occasional soft glow or interactive effect, but never too strong

Suggested color direction:
- Primary: blue-600 / blue-700 range
- Secondary: slate-800 / slate-900
- Background: white, slate-50, slate-100
- Accent: subtle indigo or sky blue if needed

## Typography
Use "Inter Tight" as the primary font.

Typography should feel:
- modern
- clean
- legible
- slightly premium

Headings:
- bold
- confident
- not overly condensed
- strong hierarchy

Body text:
- readable
- neutral
- not too small
- comfortable spacing

## Layout Principles
- generous whitespace
- strong section separation
- clear hierarchy
- consistent container widths
- balanced left/right layouts
- cards should feel intentional, not crowded
- desktop layouts should feel polished and aligned
- mobile layouts should stack cleanly and remain easy to scan

## Components
### Buttons
- strong primary CTA
- secondary CTA should be lighter but still clear
- hover states should feel smooth and premium
- no overly flashy button effects

### Cards
- soft shadow
- subtle border
- medium radius
- slight hover lift is okay
- keep card interiors spacious and readable

### Forms
- clean and minimal
- strong focus states
- easy to scan
- business-like, not playful

### Icons
- clean line icons
- consistent size and stroke
- simple, modern, professional

## Motion
Animation should be subtle and intentional.

Allowed:
- fade in on scroll
- slight upward reveal
- gentle hover lift
- soft cursor-follow or spotlight effects in selected sections
- smooth transitions

Avoid:
- bouncing
- spinning
- exaggerated parallax
- loud motion
- anything that feels gimmicky or game-like

## Images
Images should feel:
- professional
- bright
- realistic
- business-oriented
- modern office / support / consultation context

Avoid:
- dark hacker visuals
- fake corporate smiling stock photo overload
- overly dramatic cyber imagery

## Conversion Priorities
The site should always support these goals:
1. Build trust quickly
2. Clearly explain services
3. Make contacting DVIT easy
4. Encourage consultation requests
5. Present DVIT as professional and easy to work with

## Page Feel
If someone visits the site, they should think:
- "This looks professional."
- "These people seem easy to work with."
- "They understand businesses like mine."
- "This feels more premium than a typical local IT company site."
